Appraisal Services

Credible, well-supported valuations across four specialties — for lenders, attorneys, landowners, businesses, and families.

01

Agricultural

From row-crop farmland to pasture, hobby farms, and working agricultural operations, we deliver credible valuations for lending decisions, estate planning, and property transactions. Our experience spans acreages to full farm operations across the region.

02

Recreational

Hunting ground, timberland, and recreational acreages require a specialized eye. We value recreational land for its current use and its long-term potential, giving landowners and buyers a clear, defensible picture of worth.

03

Residential

Acreages, hobby farms, and rural homes each come with characteristics that set them apart from typical suburban listings. We provide accurate, well-supported residential appraisals across rural and small-town markets.

04

Commercial

From small business properties to larger commercial holdings, we bring the same market expertise, careful analysis, and professional judgment to every commercial assignment — supporting attorneys, lenders, and business owners alike.
